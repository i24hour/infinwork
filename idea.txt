Step-by-step working

When a person tells the whole procedure, or the whole kind of work they are going to do, the system makes the steps. They follow those steps and reach the target.

How it works
1. The person describes the work in their own words. That can be the full procedure, or just the kind of work and the target.
2. The system turns that description into an ordered list of steps.
   - A procedure becomes the steps they already described, in an order they can follow.
   - A kind of work becomes a path: name the target, write the finish line, do the work, check the result, and mark the target complete.
3. One step stays in front of them. Marking it done opens the next step.
4. When every step is done, the target is complete.

The first version is on the site at /steps. A plan stays in the browser, so the person can leave and come back to the same step.
